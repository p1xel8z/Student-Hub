Campus Facilities

Northbridge University provides facilities designed to support academic learning, practical education, student activities, and everyday campus life.

Library

The university library provides students with access to textbooks, reference materials, academic resources, and quiet study spaces.

Computer Laboratories

Computer laboratories provide students with systems and software required for programming, development, networking, and practical coursework.

Classrooms

Classrooms are designed to support lectures, discussions, presentations, and collaborative learning.

Sports Facilities

Students can participate in recreational and competitive activities using the university's sports and recreation facilities.

Student Activity Spaces

Dedicated spaces are available for student clubs, societies, meetings, workshops, and campus activities.

Cafeteria

The campus cafeteria provides students and staff with a convenient space for meals and refreshments.
