Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to some commonly asked questions about Northbridge University, its programs, admissions, and student life.

General Questions

Where is Northbridge University located?

Northbridge University is located in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India.

How can I contact the university?

You can contact the university by phone or email, or use the contact form available on our Contact Us page.

How can I learn more about the university?

You can visit our About page to learn more about the university.

Programs & Academics

Which programs are currently offered?

Northbridge University currently offers B.Tech programs in Computer Science and Engineering, Information Technology, and Civil Engineering.

Where can I find information about a program?

Program information is available through the Programs menu in the main navigation.

Can I learn about the faculty members?

Yes. Faculty information can be found on the Faculty page.

Campus Life

What facilities are available on campus?

The university provides facilities including classrooms, computer laboratories, a library, sports facilities, student activity spaces, and a cafeteria.

Are there student clubs and societies?

Yes. Students can participate in technical, cultural, creative, sports, and other student-led activities.

Where can I learn about campus facilities?

Visit the Facilities page for more information.

Student Services

How can I access student resources?

Student resources and external student services can be accessed through the Student Corner menu.

How do I access my student account?

Students can access their account through the Login page.

Still Have Questions?

If you cannot find the information you are looking for, please contact the university.
